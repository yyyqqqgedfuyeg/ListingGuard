# eBay Trust & Safety Internal Reviewer Manual (Internal Confidential)

## Section 1: VeRO Brand Protection & Counterfeit Enforcement Protocol

### Guideline INT-EB-301 【Trademark Infringement Investigation & Takedown SOP】
1. Keyword Manipulation Flag: Listings containing phrases such as "like Apple", "Rolex style", "Chanel replica", or "compatible with" when applied to non-accessory core products are classified as intentional VeRO violations;
2. Enforcement Hierarchy:
   - First strike: Immediate listing cancellation and VeRO notification sent to seller;
   - Second strike within 90 days: 7-day account restriction and search demotion;
   - Third strike: Permanent selling privileges revoked;
3. Counterfeit Verification: Requires inspection of OEM authorization letters and GS1 barcode database registration.

### Guideline INT-EB-302 【High-Power Laser Pointer Safety Audit SOP】
1. Risk Threshold: Any portable handheld laser labeled as burning, lighting matches, military tactical, or claiming output power exceeding 5 milliwatts (>5mW) must be immediately quarantined;
2. Mandatory Documentation: The listing must display a verifiable FDA 21 CFR accession number and manufacturer CDRH report;
3. Failure to provide regulatory proof results in immediate listing destruction and product safety strike.
